BEYOND THE BACHELOR’S

A foundation for
what follows.

The selected mathematics sequence supports your planned application to Johns Hopkins’ Robotics and Autonomous Systems program.

+

Graduate admission is separate. Hopkins expects prerequisite grades of B− or better; recognition of exam or alternative-provider credit is not established here.

01How the credits overlap+

Major, upper-division, residency, and liberal-education requirements can be met by the same courses. The map does not add another 37 upper-division or 19 residency credits. The selected science package totals 11 credits and covers the chosen Goal 2, 3, and 10 requirements.

02What the dates depend on+

Future placements follow the published recurring rotation, not confirmed registrations. Both paths assume successful study and access to the selected courses. Mathematics placement, delayed approvals, unavailable labs, or missed courses can move graduation later. May 2029 is a defined conservative scenario, not an absolute latest date.

03One elective slot, two options+

SE 482, Computer Animation and Visualization, remains the preferred elective if its prerequisites and an accessible offering are resolved. SE 450 is the scheduled fallback. Either occupies the same three-credit elective slot; they are not both required.
